Policy and field-level work New

Contributions to European and institutional discussion about AI in artistic research and higher arts education. They are inputs, not adopted policy, and I list them as such.

COST Action CA23158 · ARTinRARE

WG2 and WG4

I take part in two working groups of the COST Action on Artistic Intelligence: WG2 (collective intelligences, polylocality) and WG4 (referencing and policy). For the second general meeting (February 2026) I contributed Translating Situated AI Literacy, which offers the three lenses to WG4 as translational tools, not metrics. I also applied to contribute to WG2 with Polylocality as Method. Ongoing
Porto · 2–3 October 2026

Green Paper on AI guidelines for higher arts education

At the WG4 meeting in Porto I submitted a two-page input, From Disclosure to Agency. It argues that declarations and training records can document AI use without showing understanding or agency, and proposes contextual AI-use statements, sector-specific guidance and participatory testing of rules. Whether the Green Paper takes any of it up is not established. Input submitted
PD Arts + Creative

Shared guidelines for AI use in the PD

On 9 October 2026 I prepared a proposal for a working session on 5 November 2026: a case clinic and statement lab to draft a first set of principles with candidates, supervisors and graduate-committee members. It builds on the Porto work and is a contribution to discussion, not an adopted policy. Session planned

This work is the Change Agent’s evidence at field level, and it is also where the limit lies: contributions made, effects not yet shown. See Four Roles.
