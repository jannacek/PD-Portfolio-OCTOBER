How the final assessment runs New

As the Guideline sets it out. It is listed here so a reader can see what this portfolio is built for.

Before
The supervisory team concludes that the portfolio shows sufficient achievement of all learning goals and verifies that every required component is present. The Graduate Committee Arts & Creative then makes a formal completeness check, which is not a judgement of quality.
Committee
An examination committee is appointed on the supervisory team’s motivated proposal: an independent chair who is a lector, two lectoren independent of the trajectory, and two external professionals from the field. Members of the supervisory team cannot serve.
Portfolio review
Members read the portfolio independently, using the PD Arts + Creative Assessment Framework, and then discuss it together. Within six weeks the committee decides whether the candidate may proceed to the examination. The framework is applied holistically, not as a checklist or a scoring instrument.
Oral examination
The formal examination: a conversation with the committee on the portfolio, the research trajectory, the artistic and creative work and the learning goals, in a semi-closed or closed session. The candidate sets the format with the supervisory team and the chair.
Public presentation
Organised by the candidate for a wider audience, in a form suited to the project. It is not an assessment moment.
Archiving
The programme archives an online portfolio in the SAR Research Catalogue at the end of the trajectory. A portfolio held elsewhere, such as this site, is linked to or deposited there after the assessment. It should be a coherent, navigable whole.

During the pilot phase the Professional Doctorate cannot yet be formally conferred. Accreditation and legal recognition of the title are expected in 2028, and candidates who complete the pilot successfully before then are intended to receive the degree retrospectively.

General Guideline for the Final Assessment, version 25 September 2026, sections 3–5.
