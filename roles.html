Four Roles

The four roles of the Professional Doctorate candidate, read against what the portfolio can actually show for each.

Two frameworks

The four roles (Researcher, Innovator, Change Agent, Professional) are one assessment framework, set out by Andriessen et al. The Study Guide’s five learning goals are a second one, and they are close relatives: four of the five (Doing research, Co-creating innovations, Enabling change, Acting professionally) take over the four roles, and the fifth, Disseminating, is added. The Assessment Map shows where each goal is evidenced.

The roles are not boxes to tick. In this trajectory they interlock: the Researcher’s inquiry runs through the Change Agent’s civic interventions, and the Professional’s teaching feeds the Innovator’s method. For each role this page says what the evidence is, where to find it, and where it is thin.

I · Researcher II · Innovator III · Change Agent IV · Professional
Role I · learning goal: Doing research

Researcher

Systematic inquiry, theoretical grounding and the development of knowledge that others can use. Each AT-LAB has its own question, its own kind of evidence and a feed-forward into the next. The instruments are light and chosen for the setting: a survey (AT-LAB I), a questionnaire, curatorial reflection and group dialogue (II), an analytic autoethnography (III), a documented pipeline and dramaturgy (IV).

The strongest single piece of research evidence is the 2026 secondary analysis of AT-LAB I: 27 survey forms re-read through a frozen codebook, every closed item checked against the physical originals, borderline cases recorded and not counted, with a full audit trail. Beside it stand the methods manuscript in preparation, the peer-reviewed ISEA2026 paper, the P+ARTS contribution and the COST Action contributions.

Where it is thin. One coder, who is also the researcher. The methods manuscript is a draft for supervisors, and its co-authorship is still to be settled. The formal ethics statement for the secondary analysis is being completed.

Evidence
  • AT-LAB I secondary analysis (v1.1, 29 Sept 2026)
  • Methods manuscript and evidence ledger (October 2026 draft)
  • ISEA2026 paper, double-blind accepted · P+ARTS contribution
  • Dissemination ledger with each output’s true status
Role II · learning goal: Co-creating innovations

Innovator

Approaches with both specific and generic use. The principal innovation is the AT-LAB format: specific because every lab is built for its own place (a village hall, a master’s course, a placemaking conference, a performance space) and generic in that the structure, design, intervention, reading, reflection and feed-forward, recurs and can be re-situated. A second is the set of three lenses, a middle layer between local judgement and cross-site comparison. A third is EMI, an exploratory vocabulary for a particular need: artist-researchers inside transdisciplinary collaborations who have to say what they contribute.

ATMOS_FERA adds a fourth, an innovation in form: translational modelling, in which radiative-transfer data constrains light, sound and fog, and a live performance is argued as an epistemic instrument. The Promptare and Semiotic Protocol toolkits are innovations by design: they are written to be taken up.

Where it is thin. “Generic applicability” is designed for, not shown. Nobody else has yet run the format or the toolkit, and the ATMOS_FERA paper says the method needs recalibration for each room.

  • Promptare and Semiotic Protocol: what they support
  • ATMOS_FERA pipeline
Role III · learning goal: Enabling change

Change Agent

Starting and sustaining learning and change at individual, institutional and field level. At the community level, AT-LAB I brought wearable-sensor data and sound into a village health setting and let people say what they thought about their own data. At the institutional level, I designed and taught an AI course four times across several master’s programmes (1, 2, 2 and 1 EC), and the 2026 course is a continuation of that line. At the civic level, Promptare and the Semiotic Protocol took AI literacy into a placemaking conference and a symposium with formats built to be portable.

At the field level, I contribute to COST Action CA23158 (WG2 and WG4): a contribution on translating situated AI literacy into policy-relevant insight (February 2026) and, in Porto (2–3 October 2026), written input to a Green Paper on AI guidelines for higher arts education. A proposal for a shared guideline on AI use in the PD itself, prepared on 9 October 2026, is the newest and most local case: a working session is planned for 5 November.

Stakeholder ownership was a method throughout: students were co-inquirers rather than subjects, and the civic formats are collective meaning-making tools, not data-extraction mechanisms.

Where it is thin. The record shows contributions made, not changes that took hold. Whether the Green Paper adopts the input, or whether the course continues beyond my own teaching, is not established.

Evidence
  • Course iterations 2023–2026 · Research ↔ Teaching
  • AT-LAB III, with the public HLaS news piece on Promptare
  • COST WG2 / WG4 and AI guidelines
Role IV · learning goal: Acting professionally

Professional

Domain expertise, ethical responsibility and sustained reflection on practice. The artistic practice continued throughout: 4DSOUND training (2023), the ATMOS_FERA performance at ISO Amsterdam in October 2025, and the upcoming Akerk performance in March 2027. The teaching practice is documented across four iterations and the 2025 baseline study. Training is recorded on the Training & Curriculum page: 27.67 EC of 30, of which Sound Arguments (Orpheus Institute, Ghent, with ACPA Leiden) is the main individual strand.

Reflection is documented in the Go / No-Go (15 April 2024), in the supervision record (see Supervision), and in each lab’s feed-forward, which required looking critically at the last iteration’s limits before the next was designed. A data management plan is on file. Participant-facing formats were designed around consent and collective ownership; the ethics statement for the AT-LAB I secondary analysis is being completed.

Where it is thin. Professional networks such as ARC in the North, the V2_ AI cluster and ARIAS Artificial Worlds are self-reported on this site and have not been independently checked. The 2026 Sound Arguments year still needs its completion record.

Evidence
  • Training record and EC · Supervision
  • Evidence Archive: Go / No-Go, data management plan, certificates
