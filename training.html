How the Advances series is counted

The seminar series is worth 5 EC, however many events are attended. The six events I attended in it add up to 7.5 EC on the programme’s own list (table below), so the series is complete and the credited figure stops at 5. The other 2.5 EC are recorded as attended work and are not credited. The training form on file predates the conference elements below and still counts the series at 6 EC, so it totals 27.67 EC; it is to be updated to show the series at 5 EC and the three conference elements, which brings it to 30 EC.

Collective programme, Graduate Network

The Study Guide sets four 5-EC components, each tied to a learning goal: the seminar series Advances in Artistic and Design Research (five symposia or seminars; Doing artistic and/or design research), the module Ethics of Artistic and Design Research (Acting in a professional manner), the module Understanding Discourses & Practices of Creativity and Innovation (Co-creating innovations) and the module Complex Research Environments (Enabling change). For each module the programme issues a certificate of participation, and the candidate adds a reflection on it to the portfolio.

Teaching

Teaching is allocated 12.5% of PD time. At the Frank Mohr Institute it is also the main site of AT-LAB II, so it is not a separate activity beside the research. The course has run four times:

  • 2023: 1 EC, Discursive Context: AI in Artistic Practice (June).
  • 2024: 2 EC, Artificial Intelligence: In, Within, and Around Your Practice (April–June), across the MADTech, Painting, MAP and IRAP masters.
  • 2025: 2 EC, the third iteration, with a baseline questionnaire at the first class (N = 10).
  • 2026: 1 EC, a design course at MADTech on creative AI workflows, in three sessions.

The course iterations, what each changed, and how the research and the teaching fed one another are on the Research ↔ Teaching page.
